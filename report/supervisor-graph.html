Supervisor's graph — why two components, and what it does step by step

Four nodes, all in one process. Router and Escalation (solid orange) are Supervisor's own two components — the only real business logic it owns. Docs and Web Search (dashed) are thin call-out nodes: they just make the A2A call shown in the architecture diagram and read the result, no logic of their own. The graph itself has no loop — every path ends at Escalation or a direct answer. The one retry in this picture is internal to the Router node (it retries its own classification call up to once before failing closed), not a cycle in the graph. The blue dashed block on the left is not a node: it is the checkpointer, the one piece of state that outlives a single request. Keyed by session_id, it hands the run its prior turns on the way in and takes back whichever turn resolved the case on the way out — bounded at five, so a long conversation forgets its oldest turn rather than growing without limit.
